`sword_slash.wav`: original synthesized sword swish, generated by
`tool/generate_sword_sound.dart`. This is the active attack sound for both teams;
it contains no voice.

`genesis_music.wav` is a separate original 32-second instrumental loop with soft
chords, plucked arpeggios, and a melody. It starts on entering Genesis and plays
continuously across Creation, Eden, and the final garden, alongside the effects.
It plays at 40% volume and stops when the app is backgrounded or the story is
closed/replayed. Rebuild with `dart tool/generate_genesis_music.dart`.

`genesis_serpent_music.wav`: original suspense loop with low minor chords,
soft pulses and distant bells. The Genesis theme crossfades to this track over
2.5 seconds as the serpent's tree appears. It continues through the dialogue
and final field view; replay starts with the original Genesis theme again.
Rebuild with `dart tool/generate_serpent_music.dart`.

The active Genesis effect is `genesis_reveal.wav`, a short chime for visual
reveals. Water, wind and nature ambience loops are no longer played, including
after restart or app resume. Background music remains enabled.

The following original effect assets were generated by
`dart tool/generate_genesis_sounds.dart`:

- `genesis_waters.wav`: soft waves during the opening and creation of light.
- `genesis_wind.wav`: wind during the sky and land scenes.
- `genesis_nature.wav`: breeze and bird calls from the fifth day onward, including
  Eden and the final garden view.
- `genesis_reveal.wav`: a short chime for light, land, heavenly lights, and mankind.

Reveal cues play once per relevant dialogue step. They stop on scene exit or
app backgrounding and do not repeat on resume. The three ambience assets above
are retained as unused source assets.

`Tim Curry's devil laugh (legend).mp3`: user-supplied defeat audio. Plays once
at its original pitch alongside the laughing devil sprites from
`assets/images/devil_laugh.jpg`, while the losing screen fades in. Stops on
retry, backgrounding, or scene exit. The sprites are prepared with
`flutter test tool/prepare_devil_laugh_test.dart`.

`devil_laugh.wav`: unused synthesized alternative with a lowered male voice
and three fading echoes. Rebuild on Windows with
`powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tool/generate_devil_laugh.ps1`,
then `dart tool/mix_devil_laugh.dart`.

`warnew_battle.m4a`: the original AAC audio track extracted without re-encoding
from the supplied `assets/images/warnew.mp4`. Loops during gameplay at 65%
volume, continuing on both victory and defeat screens. Stops on backgrounding
or scene exit and resumes when returning to the app. Rebuild with
`tool/extract_battle_audio.ps1 -FfmpegPath <path-to-ffmpeg.exe>`.

`sword_attack.wav`: unused alternate sound with an offline synthesized "Yah!"
using the installed Microsoft David Desktop male voice, lowered by about five
semitones for a deeper battle cry. Only the voice is pitch-shifted; the sword
swish keeps its original pitch. No microphone recording or
downloaded voice sample is used. Playback works offline on supported platforms.

To rebuild on Windows, run `tool/generate_attack_voice.ps1` from the project root,
then `dart tool/mix_attack_sound.dart`. The intermediate voice file is in `build/`.
